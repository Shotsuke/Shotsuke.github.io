## 背景图的完整提示词

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: original panoramic illustration for a personal literary blog and a northern fantasy novel web page. Create a wide 3:2 landscape image, a wistful empty snowy Nordic coastal village in the polar blue hour, small asymmetrical wooden houses with muted dusty red, slate blue and parchment colored walls, warm tiny amber windows, sloping snow-covered street descending toward a still dark sea, pale distant island and mountain silhouettes. A small solitary red postbox near the right foreground. The street is on the right two-thirds. The left third should remain quiet pale snow and soft blue fog with very little visual detail for overlaid dark blue text. Sky is pale powder blue, faint wispy turquoise aurora near the upper right, a few tiny white snow flakes. Style: beautifully composed hand painted gouache and delicate colored pencil on textured paper, atmospheric graphic novel background, simplified architectural forms with subtle irregular hand drawn outlines, soft matte color, visible fine grain, restrained colors. Melancholy but quietly inviting, intimate human scale. No people or characters. No lettering, no text, no logo, no watermark. Avoid photorealism, 3D, glossy lighting, saturated purple, generic fantasy castles. The artwork should feel airy and light, not a dark night wallpaper.
